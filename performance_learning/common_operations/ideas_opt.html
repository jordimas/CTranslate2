CTranslate2 — CPU optimization ideas (inspired by llama.cpp)

Ranked by expected impact on the workload captured in report.html (Apple Silicon CPU; Gemm 49%, decoder GEMVs ~25%, Dequantize + BiasAdd ~7%).

# Name Potential impact Effort
1 M=1 fused int8 GEMV kernel (NEON SDOT, fuses dequant + bias + activation) 1.3–1.6× end-to-end on decoder-heavy workloads (whisper, t5gemma); eliminates ~7% from DequantizeGemmOutput + BiasAdd Medium — 1 new file, 1 dispatch hook in Gemm::operator(), NEON intrinsics, thread-local scratch for activation quant
2 SMMLA / i8mm tile GEMM for M≥8 (whisper encoder prefill) 1.5–2× on int8 whisper encoder (~27% of total runtime affected) High — new ARM i8mm kernel + interleaved weight re-pack at convert time + ISA dispatch
3 Enforce fused QKV everywhere (audit — fusion exists but not uniform) 2–3% total runtime; also reduces decode kernel-launch overhead Low — code audit in layer builders; no new kernels
4 Block-quant format (Q4_0-style, 4-bit per-32-elem block) Another 1.3–1.5× on decoder GEMV on top of #1; halves weight bandwidth again High — new quant format in model spec, converter changes, new vec-dot variants, compat matrix
5 KV-cache stored at Q8_0 / Q4_0 5–10% on long-context decode (small win on ≤30s whisper audio) Medium–high — cache layout change, attention MatMul reads quantized K/V, dequant on the fly
6 Verify Gemm epilogue fusion (BiasAdd + activation into GEMM) is active on current MPS/CPU paths Up to 3.6% + activation ops for free if currently not fused Low — verification + conditional wiring, no new kernel
